Stations are generated by `art\blender\kitbash_station.py`, in the same gunmetal-plate style as the ships ([[ship-art-direction]]). Stations don't stand for anything specific; they are just varied stations built by different factions. `--accent` is the faction colour.

What the user rejected or corrected (2026-11-04):
- **Sharp shapes:** a swept "blade" pinwheel with pointed tips was "way too sharp to be a station/habitat". Even the blunted version was dropped. Stations must read as habitable: blunt ends and chunky modules, no points.
- **Floating parts:** nav lights on dock clamps that weren't joined to their arm, and truss silos or crane arms not touching the frame. Every part must visibly connect to a truss or sit against the frame, and nav lights sit on solid metal.

Favourites picked in the visual companion (pass 3):
- ring seeds 2, 3 and 4;
- arcs seeds 1, 2 and 3;
- cross seeds 1, 2 and 3 (cross 4 was also clicked in pass 1);
- spoke seeds 2, 3 and 4;
- truss seeds 1, 3, 5 and 6.

**Why:** the user wants believable built structures, not abstract or aggressive shapes.

**How to apply:** for new station archetypes:
- connect every part with struts, collars or overlap;
- keep the ends blunt;
- show seeds side by side in the visual companion for picking.
